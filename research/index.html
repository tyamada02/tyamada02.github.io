Research

Research

My research is motivated by interpretability in machine learning. I am particularly interested in causal representation learning for time-series and other temporal data.

Causal Representation Learning I study representation learning methods that aim to recover meaningful latent structure from observed data. In particular, I am interested in causal representation learning as an approach to identifying factors that are not only predictive but also structurally interpretable.
Temporal Data Analysis I am particularly interested in time-series and other dynamically generated data. Temporal structure introduces challenges that are not captured by static data alone, and I am interested in methods that respect these dynamics while remaining interpretable.
Explainable AI A central motivation of my research is interpretability in machine learning. I am interested in designing learned representations that are easier for humans to understand, analyze, and connect to domain-relevant concepts.

Keywords

  • Causal Representation Learning
  • Temporal Data Analysis / Time-Series Data
  • Explainable AI / Interpretable Representation Learning

Current Interests

My current interest is in connecting representation learning, interpretability, and causal structure, especially in settings where data are generated over time.
