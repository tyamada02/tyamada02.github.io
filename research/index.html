Research

Research

This page outlines the main themes of my research in a compact form.

Causal Representation Learning I am interested in representation learning methods that incorporate causal structure and support more robust downstream reasoning.
Explainable AI A second focus is interpretability in machine learning, including methods that make model behavior easier to analyze and communicate.
Temporal and Dynamical Data I also work on learning problems involving sequential or dynamical data, where temporal structure is central to modeling.
