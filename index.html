    Research

    Research Themes

    Causal Representation Learning I study representation learning methods that aim to recover meaningful latent structure from observed data, with emphasis on factors that are both predictive and structurally interpretable.
    Temporal Data Analysis I am particularly interested in time-series and other temporal data, where dependencies over time create challenges that are not captured by methods designed for static data.
    Explainable AI A central motivation of my research is interpretability in machine learning. I am interested in representations that are easier for humans to understand, analyze, and connect to domain-relevant concepts.

    Keywords

    • Causal Representation Learning
    • Temporal Data Analysis / Time-Series Data
    • Explainable AI / Interpretable Representation Learning

    Current Interests

    My current interest is in connecting representation learning, interpretability, and causal structure, especially in settings where data are generated over time.
